## Diagnostic: within-country population distribution

`pop_share_l1_pct` is the total-variation distance (half the L1 distance) between the legacy and new *within-country normalised* `pop_density` vectors — i.e. the share of a country's population that sits in different cells. This is the form in which the model actually consumes population (`merge_and_adjust_population`, `src/gis_global_module.py:323-327`, renormalises `pop_density` to a within-country share and multiplies by an external country total), so it is the population statistic that can move results — any uniform per-country scale factor is cancelled exactly and cannot.

Over the 160 gated countries: median **12.29%**, p95 **29.18%**, max **35.42%** (BRA). It is **not** one of AC-3's gated metrics (AC-3 gates `pop_density` *totals*), but it is the residual most likely to move downstream numbers and is recorded here deliberately.
