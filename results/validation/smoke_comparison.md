**Both inputs come from the same scripted extraction** (`scripts/extraction/extract_grid.py`),
so the only difference between the two runs is grid resolution -- not extraction
provenance, not parameters, not worker count, not code path.

**How parameter identity was achieved.** `scripts/run_monte_carlo.py` gained a
trailing optional `--seed`, which seeds the legacy global NumPy RNG that
`src.monte_carlo_constants.sample_parameters` (and scipy's `.rvs`) draw through
-- the same mechanism `scripts/validate_lut.py` already used. Both runs were
given `--seed 20260808`. Each run logs one `PARAM_DRAW` line per draw with all
nine parameters, and they are byte-identical between the two logs:

```
diff <(grep PARAM_DRAW results/validation/smoke30_run.log) \
     <(grep PARAM_DRAW results/validation/twin5min_serial_run.log)   # no output
```

This script re-checks that diff itself and refuses to report a clean contrast if
it fails. Defaults are unchanged: without `--seed` the draws are unseeded exactly
as before.

Both runs used `--workers 1`, so per-draw parquet index **equals** draw index on
both sides and the paired section below is a true like-for-like pairing. (An
earlier 15-worker 5-arcmin twin, `results/validation/twin5min`, gives the same
medians to 3 decimal places but writes its files in completion order, so it
supports only the distribution comparison.)

## Verdict against AC-8

- **Tolerance: PASS.** Global `percent_with_water`, median across the ten draws,
  differs by **+0.018 pp** -- two orders of magnitude inside the ±2 pp bar.
- **Direction: PASS.** The walking-access shift is **+0.522 pp** on medians
  (paired mean +0.297 pp, 7 of 10 draws higher at 30as). Phase 0 predicted
  +0.46 pp more population inside the ~6.5 km walking margin at 30 arc-second;
  the model reproduces that sign and lands within 0.06 pp of the magnitude.

## The nuance worth reading before quoting these numbers

The two headline results point in *slightly* different directions, and the
paired table below is what shows it:

- `percent_piped_with_walking_access` is **higher** at 30as (7/10 draws,
  mean +0.297 pp) -- the Phase 0 effect, reproduced.
- total `percent_with_water` is marginally **lower** at 30as (9/10 draws,
  mean −0.536 pp, median −0.658 pp).

These are consistent, not contradictory: `percent_with_water` blends walking,
cycling and piped access, and the cycling components tilt the other way
(`percent_piped_with_cycling_access` mean −0.007 pp, `percent_with_only_cycling_access`
mean −0.118 pp). Phase 0 also found the resolution effect shrinking to +0.06 pp
by 10 km, i.e. essentially nothing at cycling range, so a near-zero-to-slightly-
negative cycling contribution is the expected shape.

Note that the *median-of-medians* gap on `percent_with_water` (+0.018 pp) is
smaller than the paired mean (−0.536 pp) and has the opposite sign. That is an
artefact of where the 5th and 6th order statistics fall in a 10-draw sample, not
a contradiction -- with a spread of 68.8-81.9 pp across draws, the median across
only ten draws is a noisy statistic. **The paired deltas are the trustworthy
read.** Either way every figure is far inside AC-8's ±2 pp bar, which is the
criterion actually being tested.

Two more observations, neither load-bearing for AC-8:

- 30as models 180-182 countries across the ten draws against the twin's
  179-181 -- consistently exactly **one more country**, and ~0.9M more raw
  population. Small territories that fall between 5-arcmin cell centres do get
  picked up at 30 arc-second -- the same class of effect as Phase 0's
  "144.5M coastal people silently dropped" finding, though far smaller here
  because this counts countries appearing at all, not coastal population.
- 137.9M modelled cells at 30as vs 1.42M at 5 arcmin: a 97x increase in
  resolved detail for a 0.02 pp move in the headline number. That is the
  reproducibility-and-rigour argument for the upgrade, not a change-the-answer
  argument -- exactly as the SPEC framed it.
